Frequently Asked Questions

What is this portal?
This portal is designed to report and manage issues efficiently under the Chanukya Rakshasa initiative.
How can I register?
Go to the registration page and fill in your details to create an account.
Is my data सुरक्षित (safe)?
Yes, your data is stored securely and used only for official purposes.
Can I track my complaint?
Yes, once submitted, you can track your complaint status using your ID.
Who manages this portal?
This portal is managed by the Chanukya Rakshasa Helping Portal Seva Organisation.
