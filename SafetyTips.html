Online Safety Tips

Internet has become an integral part of our daily life. With increasing use of cyberspace, cyber crimes especially against women and children are also increasing rapidly. To stay safe online, it is important to follow cyber safe practices.

1. Cyber Awareness & Hygiene for Parents

  • Talk to children about online threats like grooming, bullying and stalking.
  • Monitor online activities and set clear internet usage guidelines.
  • Watch for behaviour changes such as secrecy or excessive online activity.
  • Use strong privacy settings on social media.
  • Do not click suspicious links or attachments.
  • Cover webcams when not in use.
  • Install antivirus and parental control software.
  • Keep operating systems and software updated.
  • Use secure and updated browsers.

2. Cyber Awareness & Hygiene for Teens and Young Adults

  • Use strong privacy settings on social media.
  • Avoid accepting friend requests from strangers.
  • Block and report people who make you uncomfortable.
  • Always logout from social media after use.
  • Secure phones and devices with passwords.
  • Be cautious during video chats and calls.
  • Avoid taking or sharing sensitive photos and videos.
  • Disable location services where not required.
  • Avoid using public or free Wi-Fi for banking or shopping.

3. Cyber Awareness & Hygiene for Organizations

  • Have clear HR policies on handling illegal content.
  • Define rules for use of organisation devices.
  • Report any obscene or illegal content to police.
  • Preserve evidence securely and delete other copies.
  • Report incidents via National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.

Note: Publication, collection and distribution of Child Sexual Exploitative and Abuse Material (CSEAM) is illegal under the IT Act, 2000.
